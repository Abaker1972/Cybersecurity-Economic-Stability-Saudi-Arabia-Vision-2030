# Cybersecurity-Economic-Stability-Saudi-Arabia-Vision-2030
# Cybersecurity, Economic Stability, and Digital Transformation in Saudi Arabia

## Overview
This repository contains the dataset and supporting materials used in the empirical analysis of the study:

**“Cybersecurity in Saudi Arabia: Economic Challenges and Industry Strategies in the Age of Digital Transformation”**

The study investigates the role of cybersecurity as a structural enabler of economic stability and organizational resilience within the context of Saudi Arabia’s digital transformation under Vision 2030.

---

## Dataset Description
The dataset is based on a quantitative survey conducted among professionals and stakeholders from digitally intensive sectors in Saudi Arabia, including finance, energy, and healthcare.

The dataset includes responses related to:
- Perceived impact of cybersecurity breaches
- Adoption of advanced cybersecurity technologies
- Organizational resilience and recovery capability
- Strategic alignment of cybersecurity with Vision 2030
- Perceived economic stability

All variables were measured using a five-point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree).

---

## Methodology Summary
- Research design: Quantitative, cross-sectional
- Sample size: 419 valid responses
- Data collection method: Online survey
- Analysis techniques: Descriptive statistics, Pearson correlation, and multiple regression analysis

The dataset supports hypothesis testing related to the economic and strategic impacts of cybersecurity in a sustainability-oriented framework.
